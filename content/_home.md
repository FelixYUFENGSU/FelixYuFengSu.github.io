Hi, I'm Felix. I'm a master's student in Mathematical Sciences at NYU Tandon, and before that I studied Mathematics of Computation at UCLA.

I created this page as a digital analogue. As an avid pen and paper enthusiast, I found myself appreciating the value of having work stored online more and more. Coding is a skill that has become widely accessible due to AI. It lowered the barrier to building things but raised the bar on on judgement. True value now lies in understanding generated output and accurately discerning truth from hallucination. It is difficult to judge self worth in math when LLMs claim to have solved major proofs and even a Millenium problem. However, this is the reality that I must accept and adapt to. 

My current research interest involves applying a machine learning model to the biological processes of mice, particularly respiratory rhythm, to better understand how respiratory signals are reflected in neural activity.
